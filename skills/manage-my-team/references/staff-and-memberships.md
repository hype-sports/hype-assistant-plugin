# Team Staff invitations and league memberships

Read these workflows before preparing any related review. Follow [the complete team workflow](../SKILL.md) and [One review, one yes, all changes](../../../agents/hype.md#one-review-one-yes-all-changes); these sections add no separate approval boundary.

## 4. Invite a coach; resend or cancel a Staff invitation

Require a specific request, exact team and requested coaching title. Read `team-staff-list`, then obtain the exact address directly from the person. Check `team-invitations-list` with `query.email` for that one address. True means a pending invitation exists: explain and stop the new send (offer resend instead). Null means undetermined: explain and ask before preparing anything. False is only a momentary check, never permission.

Prepare `team-invitation-create` for email with `makeOwner: false`; never turn a coach request into an Admin offer. Explain that the title is a label, the invitation grants nothing until accepted, accepting gives Staff access including players' emergency contacts while on the current roster of an active team, and it attempts a real email that a failed send does not undo. Show the team, title, no Admin grant and the address exactly as the person typed it. Have the person confirm that address and the exact send with the same yes (no separate prompt), since a typo would invite a stranger. Then get a fresh key from `request-key-new` (input `{}`), send it as `body.idempotencyKey`, and call once. Report an invitation ID as “invited, waiting for their answer,” never “added.” Report `email: "sent"` as mail-service acceptance, not delivery, and `"not_sent"` as created but email not sent. Describe returned coaching overlaps only as returned facts.

For resend or cancel, read `team-invitations-list` without an email filter and have the person choose the exact invitation (title, expiry, last sent); addresses stay hidden, so never infer a recipient.

- **Resend** with `team-invitation-resend` only where `canResend` is true. Explain that it sends another real email and may renew an expired invitation. After a yes, get a fresh key from `request-key-new` and send it as `body.idempotencyKey`. If Hype refuses the keyed request because the invitation goes to a Hype account rather than an email address, explain and, only on a yes in the person's next message to a fresh complete review, resend without a key (in-app, no email).
- **Cancel** with `team-invitation-cancel` for a still-waiting invitation. Explain that it withdraws the offer without removing anyone already on Staff. Success is `{}`; a missing reply is unknown, never success.

Keep each key with its exact request. Reuse it, with the identical body, only when the person explicitly asks to repeat that same send after an unclear answer, after a fresh complete review of the original exact request and its duplicate-email risk and a yes in the person's next message; `email: "not_resent"` then means no new email was attempted, not proof of earlier delivery. A changed body needs a new key; a fresh key for the same address is a new send that cancels the earlier pending offer and emails again. Never mint a replacement key to recover a lost answer.

## 5. Answer an incoming Staff invitation

Use `my-team-invitations-list` only for the signed-in recipient. Have the person select the exact returned invitation, including the offered title, Admin grant if `makeOwner` is true, expiry and any returned coaching overlaps. Never answer for another person or switch accounts.

For acceptance, explain the offered Staff access, any Admin rights and emergency-contact access. For decline, explain that only this offer is declined. Show the exact invitation, team and effect; wait for explicit yes in the person's next message before `team-invitation-accept` or `team-invitation-decline` once. Acceptance returns `{ teamId }`; decline success is `{}`. Do not accept an expired offer; an expired one may still be declined. Stop on `ALREADY_RESPONDED` or any refusal; never try the other answer as a repair.

## 6. Who is waiting to join; league memberships

Clarify whether “waiting to join” means Staff invitations, follow requests, parent/guardian requests or league membership. Listing never answers anything.

- **Staff invitations:** `team-invitations-list` without an email filter (section 4).
- **Follow requests:** `team-relationship-requests-list` with `status: "pending"`; show returned names, types, statuses and dates. Followers are not players or staff. Answering follow requests is outside this skill.
- **Parent/guardian requests:** `team-guardian-requests-list` for one team. Show the player and asker names, and explain the three flags: `askedByYou` is true when this pending request is your own; `isSelfAsk` means the person approved their own ask, not that this request is yours; `askerMatchesEntryEmail` means the asker's verified sign-in email matches the parent email the team holds (no address is shown). For one exact request: `guardian-request-approve` gives that adult parent/guardian access to that player on this team, and may also link a player profile the asker offered with its family, audience and notice effects (say so, and send the person to the request in Hype if they need the exact scope first); `guardian-request-decline` closes it, tells the asker once without naming the coach, and lets them ask again; `guardian-request-block` blocks that person on this team and closes all their waiting requests here without telling them. Show the player, asker and effect, wait for a yes in the person's next message, call once. Approve returns `{ requestId, outcome }`; `already` means no new parent/guardian was added. For a whole request group by its ID, use `players-families`.
- **League memberships:** `team-memberships-list` for facts; preserve scope and unknown co-owner state, and do not treat `otherTeams` as a full league list. Changes included in the shared review: `team-membership-request` asks a league to add the team (the league must approve; returns only an ID); `team-membership-respond` with `body.accept` true or false answers a pending current-season membership from the answering side (accepting joins the league and the named division and notifies the other side; declining ends the request); `team-membership-leave` takes the team out of the league (team Admin only; keeps history; refuses during a running draft (the refusal's hint is to have the commissioner reset the draft, which cancels it, so never present that as harmless); leaving the league that created the team can end that league's co-ownership and the access it gave, so say so first); `team-membership-place-in-division` and `team-membership-remove` are League staff actions (read `league-divisions-list` for the destination). Coaching a team grants no placement authority. Success is `{}`; a missing reply is unknown, never success.
