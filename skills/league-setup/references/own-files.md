# Your own files stay where you keep them

- When the signed-in person asks you to work from their own file or keep a progress record, keep it where they say. If they have not said, ask, or use the current project folder. Never move it to a temporary, home or other folder on your own.
- A progress record holds only what the job needs to resume: steps done, Hype ids, and names the person already gave or sees in Hype. No contact details, no birth dates, no notes about children, and nothing copied from Hype that the person did not ask to keep.
- Contact details are the exception: emergency contacts and parents' or anyone else's email and phone are shown only in the answer to the person who asked. Never write them to any file, including the person's own file or progress record.
- Never publish the person's files or progress record, put them on a hosted or shared page, send them to another service or another person, commit them to a git repository the person did not choose, put real data in examples, or store passwords or tokens. The plugin keeps no server-side copy.
